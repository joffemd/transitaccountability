For years, tax measures passed here without much of a fight. Nobody showed up to argue the other side.

That has changed. We are organizing to defeat the November transit sales tax across five counties and we intend to win. While we all support functional public transit, we can no longer hand blank checks to unaccountable agencies that have burned through billions in post-pandemic aid while refusing to adapt to our new reality.

Defeating this tax is only the first step. What we are building is meant to outlast a single election. We would rather explain that part in person.

Speaking
Jon Coupal, president of the Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association. For over two decades he has led the fight to defend Proposition 13, the cornerstone of taxpayer protection that keeps people in their homes while assuring stable, predictable revenues for local governments.
Debora Allen, who recently concluded an eight-year term on the BART Board of Directors. Throughout her tenure she was a relentless advocate for fiscal accountability and transparency, consistently asking the tough financial questions the agency preferred to avoid.
Plus other special guests.

Club Auto Sport is one of Silicon Valley's most stunning event venues. Our reception takes place within their private TMFC Museum, so you will be surrounded by an incredible collection of classic cars, historical artifacts and sports memorabilia throughout the evening.
